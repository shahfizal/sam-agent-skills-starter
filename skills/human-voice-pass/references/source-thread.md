The post's hook:

> YOU CAN SPOT CHATGPT WRITING FROM A MILE AWAY.
>
> The wording is too polished. Every sentence has the same rhythm. The structure is always predictable.
>
> Here are 6 prompts that make it sound like you actually wrote it:

The six prompts, as published:

## 1. Find the tells

> "Read this text and mark every phrase that sounds AI-written: filler openers, tidy summaries, words nobody says out loud, three-item lists everywhere. Don't rewrite yet, just list them with the reason. Text: [paste]"

## 2. Write like I talk

> "Here are 3 things I wrote myself: [paste]. Study my sentence length, my words, how I open and close. Now rewrite this text in that voice. If a phrase doesn't sound like me, cut it. Text: [paste]"

His note: *"Your own samples matter more than any instruction."*

## 3. Break the rhythm

> "Rewrite this so sentence length varies: some very short, some longer. Remove any sentence that only repeats the one before it. Keep the meaning. Text: [paste]"

## 4. Cut the wrapper

> "Delete the first and last sentence of every paragraph if the paragraph still works without them. Show me what you cut."

## 5. Add what only I know

> "Ask me 5 questions about this topic that only someone with real experience could answer. Then work my answers into the text as specific details, names and numbers. Don't invent any."

## 6. The read-aloud test

> "Read this as a skeptical colleague who spots AI writing instantly. Point to every sentence you'd never hear in a real conversation and rewrite only those."

His note: *"Then read it out loud yourself once. If you wouldn't say it, change it."*

Closing:

> Use them on anything people will actually read: emails, posts, cover letters, reviews. Save this. Which tell do you spot first?

## What this skill changes

The skill generalises the six prompts into a guided editing pass. It adds:

- a fact-lock step so voice edits do not change prices, names or claims;
- a voice card built from the writer's own samples;
- a Bahasa Melayu section for natural Malaysian copy;
- output and verification formats;
- guardrails against invented experience and detector-bypass claims.

The original prompts are preserved here for attribution and reproducibility. The skill is an adaptation, not a claim of authorship over the thread.
